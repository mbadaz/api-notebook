# Code of Conduct

This project has adopted the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its Code
of Conduct. We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming
environment for everyone who takes part in the community.

The full text we follow is published here:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
(translations: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations>).

## In short

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive in all interactions.
- Welcome newcomers, give and accept feedback gracefully, and assume good
  intent.
- Harassment, discrimination, and other conduct that makes the community
  unwelcoming are not tolerated — both in project spaces (issues, pull requests,
  discussions) and when representing the project in public.

The full Contributor Covenant text linked above defines our standards and the
specific examples of acceptable and unacceptable behavior in detail.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the
maintainer at **tapsmuzira@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter
will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
take appropriate, fair corrective action — up to and including a temporary or
permanent ban — in response to behavior they consider inappropriate. The
Enforcement Guidelines we follow (Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban →
Permanent Ban) are described in the full Contributor Covenant text linked above.
